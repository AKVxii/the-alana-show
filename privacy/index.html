Privacy

A plain-language explanation of what this website collects, why it is used, and which services help operate The Alana Show online.

Last updated August 10, 2026
On this page What we collect How it is used Service providers Embedded media Security Your choices

The Alana Show collects only the information needed to respond to inquiries, manage requested email updates, operate the website, and protect the service. Information submitted through the website is not sold.

01

Information you choose to provide

Contact inquiries

The contact form may collect your name, email address, organization, phone number, inquiry type, website or social link, message, and limited page context that identifies where an inquiry began.

Newsletter signups

The newsletter form may collect a first name and email address. New signups use a confirmation flow so an address is not treated as an active subscription until the recipient confirms through the email service.

The website forms do not ask for payment-card numbers, passwords, Social Security numbers, or other similarly sensitive credentials.

02

How submitted information is used

Contact information is used to review and respond to the inquiry you send. Newsletter information is used to manage the subscription and send the updates you requested.

Submitted information is not sold. The Alana Show may use service providers to process information only as needed to operate these functions.

03

Services that help operate the site

The website currently relies on a small number of infrastructure providers:

  • Vercel hosts the website and server-side functions and may process standard technical request information needed to deliver and secure the site.
  • Resend is used to deliver contact-form inquiries to The Alana Show.
  • Kit is used to manage newsletter subscriptions and confirmation.
  • YouTube / Google supplies video content and the embedded video player.

Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices as well as the configuration used by this website.

No general advertising tracker

As of the date above, the website does not run Google Analytics or a third-party advertising pixel. Operational measurement or hosting logs may still record limited technical information needed to operate, secure, and improve the site.

04

Embedded media and external platforms

The site uses privacy-enhanced YouTube embeds where practical and links to listening, broadcast, social, guest, sponsor, and organization websites. When you play embedded media or follow an external link, the destination service may receive technical information or use its own cookies, local storage, or similar technologies under its own policies.

Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon Music, iHeartRadio, True Oldies, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, and other external destinations are not operated by The Alana Show.

05

Security and data minimization

The website uses HTTPS, keeps service credentials on the server rather than in public browser code, limits public form inputs, and applies browser and server safeguards intended to reduce abuse and unnecessary data exposure.

No internet service can promise absolute security. The goal is to collect no more than the site reasonably needs and to use proportionate safeguards for the information it handles.

06

Your choices and questions

You can unsubscribe from newsletter email using the unsubscribe option in those messages. You may also contact The Alana Show to ask about information you submitted through the website or request a reasonable correction or deletion, subject to legitimate operational or legal needs.

For privacy questions, email Alana@AlanaKVandeveer.com.
