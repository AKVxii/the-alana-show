Increase qualified discovery, viewing depth, and measurable sharing for the final George LeMieux master without re-uploading the video, changing its canonical page, manufacturing engagement, or altering approved editorial language.

## Measurement

Review these website events in Vercel Analytics and, after consent, Google Analytics:

- `Campaign Landing` — source and campaign
- `Episode Featured Moment` — which promoted segment was selected
- `Featured Play` — website player starts
- `Watch Outbound` — visitors continuing to YouTube
- `Conversation Share` — native share or copied link
- `Episode Engaged` — visible-time milestones at 30 and 120 seconds
- `Newsletter Success` — viewers entering the return-audience path

YouTube Studio remains the source for impressions, impressions click-through rate, traffic sources, and the detailed retention graph.

## Fourteen-day operating sequence

### August 26 — Infrastructure

- Deploy the short links and campaign attribution.
- Deploy the above-the-player featured moments.
- Verify the canonical episode, ordinary sitemap, and video sitemap remain unchanged.
- Preserve the current public YouTube master and URL.

Use the radio link in every spoken or written station reference:

Send the guest link individually to George LeMieux, Gunster, the LeMieux Center for Public Policy, Palm Beach Atlantic University and appropriate fellows or alumni contacts.

**Subject:** George LeMieux conversation on The Alana Show

Do not send a bulk or repetitive message. Personalize the opening line for each organization.

### August 29–September 3 — Shorts sequence

Publish one transcript-faithful Short at a time, each connected to the full interview through YouTube’s Related Video field.

1. Why Character Is Everything
2. America’s Debt and Fiscal Discipline
3. Listening Across Political Divides
4. The Hardest Political Decision
5. Just Ask George LeMieux

Each Short should:

- open immediately on George’s strongest sentence;
- use clean, accurate captions;
- avoid an extended logo introduction;
- identify George and The Alana Show without covering faces;
- end with a restrained full-conversation direction;
- use the exact real photographs in any supporting thumbnail artwork.

### September 4 — Thumbnail decision

Run YouTube Test & Compare with materially different concepts while keeping the title unchanged:

1. Current thumbnail
2. `CHARACTER IS EVERYTHING`
3. A simpler alternative such as `WHY CHARACTER MATTERS`

Use exact real photographs of Alana and George. Do not AI-regenerate either face. Let the test isolate the thumbnail; do not change the title during the same experiment.

### September 5–8 — Second wave

- Re-share the best-performing Short rather than repeating the full-video link.
- Use the strongest measured entry point in the next on-air mention.
- Ask one thoughtful Community question, not repeated engagement polls.
- Review campaign source, featured-moment selections, website engagement, YouTube impressions, CTR and retention.

## Pinned YouTube discussion prompt

> What should matter most in public leadership—character, competence, courage or results? George LeMieux explains why “character is everything” beginning at 27:49. Please share your perspective respectfully.

## Decision rules

- **Low impressions with acceptable CTR and retention:** prioritize guest, institutional, radio and Shorts distribution.
- **Healthy impressions with weak CTR:** prioritize the thumbnail test.
- **Good CTR with a sharp opening retention loss:** keep the master but lead promotion through the strongest chapter clips.
- **One featured moment consistently wins on the website:** make that moment the lead Short and next on-air hook.
- **Insufficient test volume:** preserve the current title and master; continue qualified distribution rather than forcing a premature winner.

## Guardrails

No purchased views, automated refreshing, watch exchanges, engagement pods, repetitive mass comments, misleading thumbnails, duplicate public uploads, or paid vendors promising guaranteed traffic. Preserve the final master, the canonical publication record, and the permanent episode URL.
