01 · ABOUT THE SHOW

A conversation-first South Florida media property.

Alana — All Over the Place is an independent editorial media property featuring in-depth conversations with leaders, experts, entrepreneurs, advocates, artists, athletes, public servants, public figures, and compelling everyday people. Grounded in South Florida, the show is available through radio, video, podcast platforms, and worldwide streaming.

For short references, use the name Alana — All Over the Place and link to thealanashow.com as the canonical website.
02 · HOST

Alana K. Vandeveer

Alana K. Vandeveer is an entrepreneur, commercial real estate professional, community advocate, and media host. Raised in a civically engaged Minnesota family and now based in South Florida, she brings preparation, practical judgment, warmth, and genuine curiosity to conversations about people, public life, faith, service, business, and community.
