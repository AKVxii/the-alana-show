GOOGLE & YOUTUBE DATA PRIVACY

A narrow, owner-controlled connection.

This notice applies only to Alana — All Over the Place Studio, a private owner workspace used to review Alana — All Over the Place YouTube channel. The current version is read-only and cannot publish, delete, hide, upload, or alter anything on YouTube.

Read-only scopes No advertising use No sale of data Disconnect at any time

01

Google data the console requests

The console requests only these Google OAuth permissions:

  • youtube.readonly — to view the connected channel and its video inventory.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to view non-monetary YouTube Analytics reports for that channel.

Depending on what Google makes available for the authorized channel, this may include the channel ID, channel name, handle, channel image and channel-level statistics; video IDs, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, publication dates, tags, duration, status and visibility; and analytics such as views, estimated minutes watched, average view duration, average viewed percentage, likes, comments, shares and subscribers gained.

02

How the data is used

Authorized YouTube data is used only to display the owner’s private review dashboard, identify possible duplicate or superseded uploads, organize editorial and packaging priorities, and show the current thumbnail for individual owner review.

The current console does not use Google user data to publish or modify content, target advertising, build marketing profiles, sell information, monitor unrelated browsing, or train a generalized artificial-intelligence model.

03

Storage, security and retention

The authorization session is maintained through an encrypted, Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookie. OAuth tokens are encrypted before being placed in that cookie and are not exposed to public browser scripts. The cookie may remain for up to 30 days so the owner does not have to reconnect for every review session.

The site uses HTTPS, no-cache responses, a fixed expected YouTube channel ID, CSRF state validation and PKCE during authorization. The Studio Console and its API endpoints are blocked from search indexing and public caching.

Thumbnail review decisions in this read-only phase are stored locally in the owner’s browser and can be cleared or exported. They are not sent to YouTube and cannot apply a change.

04

Service providers and sharing

Google and YouTube provide the authorized channel data. Vercel hosts the website and server-side functions and may process technical requests needed to operate and secure the console. Connected Google user data is not sold or shared with unrelated third parties for advertising or commercial profiling.

05

Owner control, revocation and deletion

The owner can use the console’s Disconnect control to revoke the Google authorization and clear the encrypted session cookie. Access can also be revoked through the Google Account page for third-party connections. Browser-stored thumbnail decisions can be cleared from the console or by clearing site data for TheAlanaShow.com.

Because the current console is read-only, disconnecting it does not remove or alter any YouTube video, caption, playlist, thumbnail, title, description, visibility setting or monetization setting.

06

Policies and questions

Use of information received from Google APIs is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and with the YouTube API Services Terms and Developer Policies.

Questions or requests concerning this connection may be sent to Alana@AlanaKVandeveer.com.
