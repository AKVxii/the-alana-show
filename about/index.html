Raised in a civically engaged Minnesota family and now based in South Florida, Alana is drawn to the people behind the title—the experience, conviction, choices, setbacks, service, and ideas that make a conversation worth hearing.

01

Experience beyond the microphone.

Alana K. Vandeveer is an entrepreneur, commercial real estate professional, community advocate, and media host. That mix of business, community involvement, and public-facing work informs a style of interviewing that is practical without being transactional and curious without being performative.

Her interests cross business, leadership, public service, faith, wellness, technology, culture, community, and the people who step forward when something needs to be done.

02

Preparation first. Conversation second.

The goal is not to force every guest into the same format. Alana approaches interviews by learning enough beforehand to ask better questions, then listening closely enough to follow the conversation somewhere more useful.

Guests include leaders, experts, entrepreneurs, advocates, artists, athletes, public servants, public figures, and compelling everyday people. An appearance is an opportunity for a substantive conversation—not an automatic endorsement of every view associated with the guest.

“If someone isn’t in the room, don’t talk about them.”

A principle behind every conversation
